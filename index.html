The Craft

Extracted cold,
bottled with precision.

Within hours of harvest, olives are cold-extracted at controlled temperatures — preserving the polyphenols, the aroma, and the character that makes each batch distinct.

I
Harvest to Press
Olives are pressed within 24 hours of harvest, capturing peak freshness and minimal acidity.
II
Cold Extraction
Temperatures never exceed 27°C — ensuring maximum polyphenol retention and flavour integrity.
III
Sealed & Documented
Each bottle is batch-labelled, lab-tested, and stored under controlled conditions before dispatch.
27° Max extraction temp.
0% Cross-batch mixing

Preservation

Stored with the
same discipline.

After extraction, every batch is transferred to temperature-controlled stainless steel vessels — preserving the polyphenols, the aroma, and the integrity of each harvest until the moment of bottling.

I
Temperature Control
Storage conditions maintained below 18°C — protecting polyphenol content and long-term flavour stability.
II
Inert Atmosphere
Nitrogen-blanketed vessels prevent oxidation — the oil arrives as fresh as the day it was pressed.
III
Batch Integrity
Each harvest stored separately. No cross-batch mixing, ever.

From Grove to Bottle

I

Hand-selected olives from long-established Tunisian groves, harvested at precise ripeness each season.
