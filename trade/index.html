Trade Programme

For Distributors
& Retailers

We work selectively with parties who share our commitment to provenance. Our trade programme is designed for distributors, retailers, and culinary professionals who understand that olive oil is an ingredient — and want to offer their clients the best version of it.

Minimum order quantities apply. All trade orders ship with full batch documentation, certificates of analysis, and harvest records.

Certifications

Backed by documentation.

IOC Certified
Compliant with International Olive Council standards for extra virgin classification. Acidity, peroxide, and spectrophotometric values all within grade.
EU Organic Certified
Our Organic range is fully EU Organic certified. Certificate of conformity available with every trade order. No synthetic pesticides or fertilisers at any stage.
Lab Verified
Every batch is independently tested for polyphenols, acidity, peroxide value, and wax content. Full certificate of analysis supplied with each shipment.
